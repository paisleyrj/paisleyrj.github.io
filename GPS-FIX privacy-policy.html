FIX

Privacy Policy · Effective September 23, 2026

FIX is a simple GPS snapshot tool for iPhone. It shows local time, latitude, longitude, and estimated altitude when you press the shutter. This policy describes what the app does with that information.

Short version

Location is used only on your device, only when you take a snapshot. FIX does not upload, sell, or share your coordinates. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or third-party trackers in this build.

1. Information the app uses

When you press the shutter, FIX may access:

  • Location — latitude, longitude, horizontal accuracy, and GPS altitude (if available), via Apple’s Core Location.
  • Time — the device clock and time zone for the plate timestamp.
  • Preferences — metric or imperial units, stored only on your device.

Location access is limited to While Using the App. FIX does not request Always / background location, and it does not continuously track you.

2. How information is used

  • To display the snapshot plate (time, coordinates, altitude, accuracy).
  • To let you copy or share text you choose to export via the system share sheet.

GPS altitude is an estimate from the device and is not a surveyed or barometric measurement. FIX labels it accordingly in the interface.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not collect location or other personal data on our servers.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash reporters that transmit data off-device in this build.
  • We do not require an account or login.

If you use the system Share sheet, any app or service you pick is governed by that product’s own privacy policy—not by FIX.

4. Data storage and retention

Snapshot plates and unit preference live on your device (in memory and local preferences). Uninstalling the app removes them. We do not maintain a remote copy.

5. Children’s privacy

FIX is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way (for example if a future version added optional cloud features), we will update this page and the effective date.
